Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Who We Are

Sidlytix provides websites, dashboards, data management and automation services and products to businesses worldwide. We work fully online. You can reach us at hello@sidlytix.com.

Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website form, email or WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Name and email address
  • Phone or WhatsApp number (optional)
  • Country and industry
  • The service or product you are interested in
  • Any business information you choose to share in your message

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses a third-party icon library, so these providers may receive basic technical information such as your IP address when you visit.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide quotes
  • To deliver, set up and support the services and products you request
  • To communicate with you about your project
  • To improve our website and services

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Legal Basis (EU/UK Visitors)

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we process your information to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, to perform a contract with you, and for our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and running our business.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Enquiries submitted through our website are stored in Google Sheets within our Google Workspace account. This means your information may be stored on servers outside your country. We take reasonable measures to protect it.

Data Processed Through Our Products

Products such as our Email Summary System are installed inside the client's own Google account. Emails are processed within that account, and summaries are saved to Google Sheets owned by the client. We only access a client's account during setup or support, and only with their permission. We do not keep copies of client emails on our own servers.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep enquiry information for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless we need it longer for an ongoing project or legal reasons. After that, it is deleted.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Raise a complaint with your local data protection authority

To make a request, email hello@sidlytix.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Cookies

Our website does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. If this changes, we will update this policy.

External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites, including WhatsApp and LinkedIn. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us through the website form or email hello@sidlytix.com.
