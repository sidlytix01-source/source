01 Education & Training

Admissions, fees and results, without the end-of-term scramble.

For schools, coaching institutes and training centres that still chase fees on WhatsApp and rebuild the same reports every term.

What usually goes wrong

  1. Fee dues live in registers and phone notes, so follow-ups slip through.
  2. Parents call the office for things the website should answer: timings, fees, admission dates.
  3. Attendance and results are typed twice, once into a sheet and again into a report.
